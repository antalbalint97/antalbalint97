### Research

Today's models represent knowledge badly: what a claim means depends on its context and on what else is known, but embedding similarity flattens that. I work on a formal model where knowledge activates context-dependently and every claim is versioned as the evidence behind it changes.

On top of it I'm building toward **science automation**: a system that tracks the state of a field - claims, evidence, open questions - and decides what to investigate next. The first planned test is retrospective: given only the literature up to a cutoff year, can it propose research questions the field later pursued?
